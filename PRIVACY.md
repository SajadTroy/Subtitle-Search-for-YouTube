# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 29, 2026

## Overview
"Subtitle Search for YouTube" was built with user privacy as its core foundation. We believe your data belongs to you.

## Data Collection
**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.** 

- **No Tracking:** We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party tracking software.
- **No Servers:** We do not own or operate any servers. All processing happens locally on your machine.
- **No Telemetry:** We do not collect crash reports or usage statistics.

## How it Works
The extension requests permissions strictly to interact with `youtube.com`. When you open a YouTube video, the extension reads the publicly available subtitle track provided by YouTube and displays it in a search interface.

When you search for a word, the search algorithm runs entirely locally within your browser. **No data ever leaves your computer.**

## Changes to This Policy
If we make any changes to this Privacy Policy in the future, we will update this document and provide clear notice within the extension's changelog.

## Contact
If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the project's repository.
